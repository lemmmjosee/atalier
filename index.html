About the project

Atelier Métamorphe is a free learning space for students and first-time sewists who want fewer garments that do more. Patterns are drafted to use simple rectangles and circles, so you can learn construction without expensive tools or complicated fittings.

Stitched with care · Patterns are free for personal and classroom use
