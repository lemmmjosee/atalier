Why transformable?

Fewer clothes, more outfits

A transformable garment is cut and tied so that one piece can be worn several ways. A single wrap becomes a halter top, a skirt or a dress. You sew once and style many times.

    Measurement guide

    How to measure

    1. A Bust: around the fullest part of the chest.
    2. B Waist: around the narrowest part of the torso.
    3. C Hip: around the fullest part of the hips.
    4. D Shoulder width: from one shoulder point to the other, across the back.
    5. E Garment length: from the shoulder or waist down to the hem you want.
    6. F Arm circumference: around the fullest part of the upper arm.

    Keep the tape snug but not tight, and measure over thin clothing. Compare your numbers with the table and choose the size that fits your largest measurement. For stretch fabrics, choose the smaller size. Your numbers are never stored.
